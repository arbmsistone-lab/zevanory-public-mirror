# EG-0064 — ARBM SIST V10 Microsoft Store readiness

Date: 2026-09-01
Decision scope: Store listing, privacy, screenshots and MSIX submission readiness.

## Independent evidence
1. Microsoft Store Policies v7.19: privacy policy is required when a product accesses, collects or transmits personal information; policy must disclose access, use, storage, security, disclosure and user controls.
   Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/store-policies
2. Microsoft Store MSIX listing guidance: at least one screenshot is required and four or more are recommended; listing information must accurately describe the app.
   Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/publish-your-app/msix/add-and-edit-store-listing-info
3. Microsoft Store MSIX certification guidance: run WACK locally, test across environments, disclose prerequisites and provide certification notes for special requirements.
   Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/publish-your-app/msix/app-certification-process
4. Microsoft Store distribution guidance: Store-distributed MSIX packages are signed by Microsoft after certification; a separately purchased CA-trusted certificate is not required for Store submission.
   Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/get-started
