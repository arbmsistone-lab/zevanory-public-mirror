# ZEA-10 Autonomy Engine

## Mission

ZEA-10 is the operational excellence and autonomy engine for ZEVANORY.

Its primary purpose is **not external certification**. Its purpose is to continuously prove that ZEVANORY can execute the complete commercial lifecycle with elite engineering quality, while keeping the human owner in an administrative/approval role.

External reviews may exist as optional evidence, but they never block internal engineering progress.

## North Star

A controlled lead can enter ZEVANORY and traverse:

market intelligence -> segmentation -> campaign -> creative -> human approval when policy requires -> publication -> lead identity -> memory/context -> conversation -> objection handling -> offer -> checkout -> payment confirmation -> fulfillment -> onboarding -> support -> satisfaction -> retention -> learning -> next campaign

without routine human operation.

## Authority model

- **AUTO**: research, segmentation, lead context, memory, nurturing, support, follow-up, analysis, optimization.
- **AUTO_GATED**: channel publication, offer delivery, recovery, checkout handoff when policy gates are satisfied.
- **HUMAN_APPROVAL**: sensitive/high-impact creatives, material price/offer changes, high-risk campaigns, policy exceptions.
- **HUMAN_ONLY**: legal disputes, fraud, irreversible financial exceptions, contractual exceptions, low-confidence/high-risk cases.

## Green definition

ZEA-10 is GREEN only when all 10 pillars are simultaneously proven on one immutable candidate SHA by:
1. source capability evidence;
2. reproducible automated checks;
3. live control-plane health;
4. deterministic synthetic commercial journey;
5. fail-closed commercial safety;
6. explicit human-approval boundaries;
7. no mandatory paid provider for the core path.

A blocked real-world sales channel does not erase engineering capability, but the system must expose that channel as unavailable and refuse unsafe execution.
